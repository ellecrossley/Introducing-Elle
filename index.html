Introducing Elle Crossley!!

Hi, I'm Elle! I'm a Brooklyn-based student journalist at the Craig Newmark Graduate School of Journalism specializing in audio reporting.

I have previous experience working at KUER, NPR Utah, as a general assignment reporter, covering a variety of topics concerning communities throughout my home state. Here's a few examples of my favorite audio/print package pieces:

  • As homeless rates climb, the Street Dawg Crew helps Utahns by supporting their pets: A non-profit pet food pantry found a unique approach to aid Utah’s growing homeless crisis. They hand out thousands of pounds of pet food at Salt Lake City’s Pioneer Park every other week to help those experiencing financial hardship to keep their furry friend’s unconditional love in their lives.
  • TikTok’s Ilona Maher is supercharging Utah girls rugby — and players’ confidence: High school girls rugby saw significant growth in the United States after the USA Women’s Rugby Sevens team took home the bronze medal in the 2024 Paris Olympics. In Utah, players say rugby star Illona Maher is boosting their sport and their confidence.
  • Utah’s LGBTQ+ community is anxious about the rhetoric after Charlie Kirk’s death: News of Charlie Kirk’s alleged assassin’s romantic relationship with a transgender woman triggered a surge of anti-transgender discourse on social media. LGBTQ+ mental healthcare providers in Utah said they saw significant increased demand for their services as queer communities felt increasingly unsafe. .
